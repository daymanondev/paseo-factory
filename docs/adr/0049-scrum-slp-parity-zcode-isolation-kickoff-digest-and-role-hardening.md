# 0049 — Agile Scrum Parity with SLP: ZCode Clean-Room Isolation, Unified Seat Kickoff & Compact Run Digest, Denylist Helper MCP Scoping, and Role-Split Operational Hardening

- **Date:** 2026-11-05
- **Status:** Accepted — Owner confirmed Q1–Q4 in `/grill-with-docs` ("toàn bộ theo bạn khuyến nghị, không triển khai luôn ở session này nha").
- **Amends:** [ADR 0032](0032-scrum-team-execution-mode.md) & [ADR 0034](0034-peer-context-handoff-scrum-run-budgets-and-ui-approval-checkpoint.md) (aligns Agile Scrum orchestration, kickoff, handoff, and specialist prompts with SLP's operational laws through `v0.1.0`) · [ADR 0039](0039-unified-peer-helper-mcp-catalog-oauth-sync-and-connectivity-verification.md) & [ADR 0042](0042-unified-skill-hub-host-ecosystem-discovery-and-project-skill-packs.md) (replaces Scrum's hardcoded Helper MCP regex allowlist with full Owner-synced catalog pass-through plus a visual/browser denylist for `pm` and `architect`, and wires `helperMcpSources` into `provisionScrumHarness`) · [ADR 0045](0045-per-project-ledger-isolation-compact-migration-and-scoped-retro.md) (routes `bindProjectDirective` through the per-Project `Factory` instance rather than the root `Factory`) · [ADR 0046](0046-zcode-acp-harness-integration-clean-room-home-and-token-metering.md) (isolates Scrum ZCode seat and specialist homes under `<projectStateDir>/scrum/zcode-home/...` with `renderScrumPrompt` in `ROLE_SYSTEM.md` and role-scoped skills) · [ADR 0047](0047-lead-context-budget-hygiene-active-only-roster-and-peer-owned-skills.md) & [ADR 0048](0048-in-flight-wave-hardening-recurrence-guard-and-project-notebook.md) (unifies Scrum Turn-1 kickoff onto `buildSeatKickoffPacket` with `omitSystemPromptDuplicates: true`, `<projectStateDir>/notebook.md`, and a bounded `formatScrumRunDigest(activeRun)`, and maps 3-tier wave hardening and `Recurrence Guard` across `Scrum Lead`, `Senior Dev`, and `QA Engineer`).

## Context

Between `v0.0.52` ([ADR 0032](0032-scrum-team-execution-mode.md)) and `v0.0.81` ([ADR 0041](0041-gui-open-design-project-link-rpc-studio-relay-and-windows-runtime-parity.md)), `paseo-factory` introduced **Agile Scrum** (`mode: "scrum"`). From `v0.0.84` through `v0.1.0` ([ADR 0042](0042-unified-skill-hub-host-ecosystem-discovery-and-project-skill-packs.md) → [ADR 0048](0048-in-flight-wave-hardening-recurrence-guard-and-project-notebook.md)), **SLP** (`mode: "slp"`) underwent intensive live-run hardening. While Scrum automatically inherited shared lower-level runtime upgrades (Choke non-interpreter heredoc stripping and state-dir env expansion, Quiet-Point Lead Succession and dual-trigger drain, server-side `Task worktree` allocation, worktree process-group reaping, and multi-provider token metering), Scrum diverged in its separate harness provisioning, Turn-1 kickoff path, `128k` handoff templates, and role prompts (`src/scrum/prompts.ts`), leaving 6 concrete regressions/bugs and missing SLP's newer operational defenses:

1. **ZCode Clean-Room Loss of Scrum Prompt & Role Skills ([ADR 0046](0046-zcode-acp-harness-integration-clean-room-home-and-token-metering.md)):**
   - In `plugin/server/roles.ts` (`factoryEnvFor`), spawning any Scrum seat or specialist (`pm`, `ux`, `architect`, `dev`, `qa`) on a `zcode/*` lane calls `provisionZcodeHome` without passing `systemPrompt: renderScrumPrompt(...)`. Inside `src/zcode-home.ts` (`writeRoleContract`), an omitted `systemPrompt` falls back to `ROLE_PROMPTS[role]` — the **SLP** role contract (`renderRoleAppendSystem`). Because Paseo's `ACPAgentSession` does not forward `config.systemPrompt` over ACP to `zcode.cjs`, every Scrum seat or specialist on `zcode/*` loses its Scrum identity (`PM`, `UX`, `Architect`, `Dev`, `QA`, `Scrum Lead`) and runs under the SLP contract. Furthermore, `zcode-home.ts` shares `<stateDir>/zcode-home/{supervisor,lead}` globally across modes and provisions SLP skill sets (`CORE_LEAD_SKILLS` vs all skills) instead of `resolveRoleSkillsForProject`.
2. **Turn-1 Context Bloat, Duplicate System Prompt, Raw `ScrumRun` JSON Dump, and Hallucinated Kickoff Reference ([ADR 0047](0047-lead-context-budget-hygiene-active-only-roster-and-peer-owned-skills.md), [ADR 0048](0048-in-flight-wave-hardening-recurrence-guard-and-project-notebook.md)):**
   - In `plugin/server/lead-mailbox.ts` (`prependKickoffPacketIfFirstTurn`), `selection.mode === "scrum"` bypasses `buildSeatKickoffPacket` (`src/preflight.ts`) and instead re-injects `renderScrumPrompt` (already present in `config.systemPrompt`) plus `Live Scrum state: ${JSON.stringify(activeRun)}`.
   - As a Scrum run progresses across 15–24 tasks, `JSON.stringify(activeRun)` dumps tens of kilobytes of raw `operationIntents`, `contractIntents`, `artifacts`, and `wakeups` into Turn 1.
   - Worse, `src/scrum/prompts.ts` instructs seats to *"Check the Seat kickoff packet before reading memory files"*, yet Scrum seats never receive `buildSeatKickoffPacket` (missing the `<50ms` deterministic repo preflight, detected scripts, absent-memory-file inventory, and `<projectStateDir>/notebook.md`).
3. **Missing `helperMcpSources` and Hardcoded Regex Stripping of Custom Helper MCPs ([ADR 0039](0039-unified-peer-helper-mcp-catalog-oauth-sync-and-connectivity-verification.md)):**
   - `provisionScrumHarness` (`src/scrum/harness.ts`) calls `provisionPiAgentHome` without passing `helperMcpSources: { userHome }`.
   - `scopeScrumCapabilities` filters `config.mcpServers` via a hardcoded regex (`/context7|exa|search|research|vision|chrome|playwright|browser|open-design|open_design|expo|supabase|firebase/i`), silently stripping any other Owner-synced server in `<stateDir>/helper-mcps.json` (e.g., `sequential-thinking`, `notion-mcp-server`, `science_*`, `github`, `linear`).
4. **Root `deps.factory` Passed Instead of `projectFactory(slug)` on Scrum Directive Admission ([ADR 0045](0045-per-project-ledger-isolation-compact-migration-and-scoped-retro.md)):**
   - In `plugin/server/lead-mailbox.ts` (`dispatchSupervisor`), `bindProjectDirective(deps.factory, root, slug, request)` passes the root `deps.factory` (whose `ledger.jsonl` is empty after per-Project ledger isolation) instead of `projectFactory(slug)`.
5. **SLP-Only `128k` Lead Handoff Templates in Scrum:**
   - When Scrum Lead crosses `128,000` tokens, `renderHandoffExtractionPrompt` and `renderSuccessorKickoff` use SLP-only sections without extracting or summarizing the active Scrum run's phase, track, readiness, design decision, QA audits, fix reservations, or pending Owner requests.
6. **Live-Run Failure Guards Missing from `src/scrum/prompts.ts`:**
   - While `src/roles-contract.ts` grew to 281 lines of battle-tested operational laws, `src/scrum/prompts.ts` remained 37 lines, lacking the Supervisor First-Action Tool Call Law ([ADR 0029](0029-delegation-boundary-rolling-waves-and-challenge-reconciliation.md)), Lead Peer-Owned Domain Skills Law and Bounded Per-File Diff Review ([ADR 0047](0047-lead-context-budget-hygiene-active-only-roster-and-peer-owned-skills.md)), Proof Falsification and Out-of-Band Script Ban ([ADR 0044](0044-vertical-wave-slicing-contract-bounded-review-reply-first-handoff-heredoc-choke-and-worktree-process-reaping.md)), Context-Ceiling Recovery (`peer-handoff-<task>.md`, [ADR 0035](0035-peer-context-handoff-and-wave-redecomposition.md)), Anti-Whack-a-Mole Law, and [ADR 0048](0048-in-flight-wave-hardening-recurrence-guard-and-project-notebook.md)'s `Recurrence Guard`, `Wave Hardening`, `Project notebook`, and 3-tier verification hierarchy.

## Decision

### 1. Isolated Scrum ZCode Harness Homes & Role-Scoped Skills (`src/zcode-home.ts`, `plugin/server/roles.ts`)

1. **Immutable Per-Seat & Per-Task-Epoch ZCode Paths for Scrum:**
   - Mirroring `provisionScrumHarness` (`src/scrum/harness.ts`), when a Scrum seat or specialist runs on a `zcode/*` lane, its clean-room `ZCODE_STORAGE_DIR` / `ZCODE_SESSION_DB_PATH` is provisioned under `<projectStateDir>/scrum/zcode-home/`:
     - Standing seats (`supervisor`, `lead`): `<projectStateDir>/scrum/zcode-home/seats/<selectionRevision>/<seatGeneration>/<authority>/`
     - Specialists (`pm`, `ux`, `architect`, `dev`, `qa`): `<projectStateDir>/scrum/zcode-home/runs/<runId>/<task>/<epoch>/`
   - Placing Scrum ZCode homes under `<projectStateDir>/scrum/zcode-home/` guarantees zero collision with SLP's `<stateDir>/zcode-home/{supervisor,lead,tasks}` and ensures `Project reset` / `Project delete` (`removeProjectStateDirectory`) purges Scrum ZCode homes automatically.
2. **Scrum Role Contract & Role-Scoped Skills in ZCode:**
   - `factoryEnvFor` passes the rendered Scrum prompt (`renderScrumPrompt(...)`, including any linked Open Design contract and task artifact references) as `systemPrompt` into `provisionZcodeHome`, which writes it to `<roleHome>/ROLE_SYSTEM.md` referenced by `instructions.userFile`.
   - `provisionZcodeHome` accepts an explicit `allowedSkills?: readonly string[]` list (resolved via `resolveRoleSkillsForProject(role, stateDir, slug, root, contractSkills)`) so each Scrum specialist on `zcode/*` receives its exact role-scoped skill symlinks (plus `Supervisor` retaining 100% of host skills except `/factory` when applicable).

### 2. Unified Turn-1 `Seat kickoff packet`, `<projectStateDir>/notebook.md`, Compact `formatScrumRunDigest`, and Per-Project Directive Binding (`src/preflight.ts`, `plugin/server/lead-mailbox.ts`)

1. **Unified `buildSeatKickoffPacket` for Scrum (`omitSystemPromptDuplicates: true`):**
   - `prependKickoffPacketIfFirstTurn` (`plugin/server/lead-mailbox.ts`) routes both `slp` and `scrum` modes through `buildSeatKickoffPacket` (`src/preflight.ts`) with `omitSystemPromptDuplicates: true` and `mode: selection.mode`:
     - Eliminates the duplicate Turn-1 injection of `renderScrumPrompt` and `OPEN_DESIGN_CONTRACT` (already present in `config.systemPrompt` / `APPEND_SYSTEM.md` / `ROLE_SYSTEM.md`).
     - Gives Scrum `Supervisor` and `Scrum Lead` the `<50ms` deterministic repository preflight (git branch, `HEAD`, dirty status, detected `package.json` / build scripts, top-level files, existing vs absent `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md` / `CONTEXT.md` / `.scratch` inventory), the mode-scoped `paseo_factory` MCP tool table (`SCRUM_SUPERVISOR_MCP_TOOLS` / `SCRUM_LEAD_MCP_TOOLS`), and `<projectStateDir>/notebook.md` (`Verification & Run Recipe` + `Recurrence Guard`).
2. **Bounded `formatScrumRunDigest(activeRun)` (Replaces Raw `JSON.stringify(activeRun)`):**
   - Replaces `Live Scrum state: ${JSON.stringify(activeRun)}` on Turn 1 and during `128k` `Lead handoff` with a deterministic, compact Markdown digest (`formatScrumRunDigest(activeRun)`, ~300–500 tokens) covering:
     - Run identity & lifecycle: `runId`, `initiativeId`, `phase`, `track` (`rationale`), `configRevision`, and remaining `budgets` (`maxTasks`, `tasksUsed`, `maxFixRounds`, `fixRoundsUsed`).
     - Readiness & UI Design status: `readiness` verdict/blockers and `design` revision, digest, decision, and screen statuses.
     - Active / unaccepted Scrum Tasks in full detail (`task`, `role`, `status`, `epoch`, `artifactRefs`) with already-settled Tasks compacted to a single summary line.
     - Active `qaAudits` (latest audit verdict, defects, cold-start/integration proof status), `fixReservations`, pending `ownerRequests`, and undelivered `wakeups`.
3. **Scrum-Aware `128k` `Lead handoff`:**
   - When `readExecution(stateDir, slug).mode === "scrum"`, `renderHandoffExtractionPrompt` instructs the outgoing `Scrum Lead` to summarize current Sprint stage progress, readiness/design blockers, and next specialist dispatches without repeating raw artifact contents, and `renderSuccessorKickoff` embeds `formatScrumRunDigest(activeRun)` alongside `<projectStateDir>/notebook.md` and the active Peer roster.
4. **Per-Project `Factory` on Directive Admission:**
   - `dispatchSupervisor` (`plugin/server/lead-mailbox.ts`) passes `projectFactory(slug)` (not the root `deps.factory`) to `bindProjectDirective(projectFactory(slug), root, slug, request)`.

### 3. Denylist-Based `Helper MCP catalog` Scoping for Scrum Specialists (`src/scrum/harness.ts`)

1. **Wire `helperMcpSources` into `provisionScrumHarness`:**
   - `provisionScrumHarness` passes `helperMcpSources: { userHome: input.userHome ?? homedir() }` to `provisionPiAgentHome` so Scrum specialist Pi homes always sync the latest Owner-enabled Helper MCP servers and OAuth token bundles from `<stateDir>/helper-mcps.json`.
2. **Replace Hardcoded Regex Allowlist with Visual/Browser Denylist for `pm` and `architect`:**
   - `Supervisor` and `Scrum Lead` remain strictly isolated to `paseo_factory` alone.
   - Working Scrum specialists (`pm`, `ux`, `architect`, `dev`, `qa`) receive **all** Owner-enabled Helper MCP servers from `<stateDir>/helper-mcps.json` across both Pi (`mcp.json`) and Claude/Codex/OpenCode (`scopeScrumCapabilities` on `config.mcpServers`), **except** visual, browser, and UI-canvas inspection servers (`/^(?:zai-vision|zai-mcp-server|chrome-devtools(?:-mcp)?|playwright(?:-mcp)?|open-design|open_design)$/i`), which are withheld from text/architecture planning roles (`pm` and `architect`) and retained for `ux`, `dev`, and `qa`.

### 4. Role-Split Operational Hardening in `src/scrum/prompts.ts`

To bring Scrum's prompts to full parity with `src/roles-contract.ts` while honoring Scrum's 5-stage BMad V7 role separation (`Supervisor` ⇄ `Scrum Lead` → `PM` → `UX` ∥ `Architect` → `Senior Dev` ⇄ `QA Engineer`), operational laws are mapped by role:

1. **Scrum Supervisor (`supervisor`):**
   - **First-Action Tool Call Law ([ADR 0029](0029-delegation-boundary-rolling-waves-and-challenge-reconciliation.md)):** `Scrum Lead` is a separate process and cannot read Supervisor chat replies; when dispatching or relaying an Owner decision, Supervisor must invoke `supervisor_dispatch_lead` or `supervisor_scrum_decision` as its immediate first action and end its turn.
   - **Diagnostics & Run-Verification Law ([ADR 0019](0019-supervisor-diagnostics-law-worktree-retention-and-opencode-shim-delegation.md)) & Outcome/Architecture Boundary ([ADR 0029](0029-delegation-boundary-rolling-waves-and-challenge-reconciliation.md)):** Inspect read-only (`ls`, `package.json`, `git status`, `git log`) before dispatching repair directives; challenge single-file consolidation anti-patterns before dispatching.
   - **4-Line Owner Ratification & Run Completion Summary ([ADR 0048](0048-in-flight-wave-hardening-recurrence-guard-and-project-notebook.md)):** Present final run completion in the scannable 4-bullet summary (*1. Built/Fixed, 2. Verification & Cold-Start Proof, 3. How to Run, 4. Residual Risks*) and any `Recurrence Guard` repo-memory proposals in the 4-line ratification format.
2. **Scrum Lead / Scrum Master (`lead`):**
   - **Peer-Owned Domain Skills Law ([ADR 0047](0047-lead-context-budget-hygiene-active-only-roster-and-peer-owned-skills.md)):** Never read (`cat`) implementation or framework `SKILL.md` files (`expo-*`, `firebase-*`, `modern-web-guidance`, etc.) into `Scrum Lead`'s context window; specialists load their own role-scoped skills in their worktrees.
   - **Concise `<= 2,500`-Char 4-Part Briefs ([ADR 0047](0047-lead-context-budget-hygiene-active-only-roster-and-peer-owned-skills.md)):** Keep `Contract.description` under `2,500` chars with Bounded Outcome, Contract Boundary & Regression Invariants, Excluded / Deferred Scope, and Verification Gate.
   - **Bounded Per-File Diff Review (`<= 6,000` Tokens), Proof Falsification, & Out-of-Band Script Ban ([ADR 0044](0044-vertical-wave-slicing-contract-bounded-review-reply-first-handoff-heredoc-choke-and-worktree-process-reaping.md), [ADR 0047](0047-lead-context-budget-hygiene-active-only-roster-and-peer-owned-skills.md)):** Inspect `git show --stat "<sha>"` first and review `git diff "<sha>^" "<sha>" -- <file>` one file at a time without `JSON.stringify` wrapping; before `lead_accept_task` on `dev` or `qa` tasks, verify **Proof Falsification** (*"If the claimed behavior disappeared, would the specialist's test fail?"*); never run ad-hoc `node -e` / `python3 -c` scripts to invent uncontracted edge cases after a GREEN Gate.
   - **Context-Ceiling Recovery (`160k`, [ADR 0035](0035-peer-context-handoff-and-wave-redecomposition.md)):** When a specialist task hits `BLOCKED (context-ceiling)`, read `<projectStateDir>/peer-handoff-<task>.md` and the worktree's committed progress, then split the remaining work into a narrower follow-up story rather than re-spawning the same oversized scope.
   - **In-Flight `Wave Hardening`, `Recurrence Guard`, and `<projectStateDir>/notebook.md` ([ADR 0048](0048-in-flight-wave-hardening-recurrence-guard-and-project-notebook.md)):** Maintain `## 1. Verification & Run Recipe` and `## 2. Recurrence Guard` (`observed → recurred → hardened → verified`) in `<projectStateDir>/notebook.md`, and enforce the 3-part Recurrence Guard (root-cause pattern + sibling sweep across peer screens/modules + regression test) when contracting `fix_cycle` tasks.
3. **Senior Dev (`dev`):**
   - **Tier-1 Slice Self-Check, Modular Test Suite (`tests/<slice>.*`), & Explicit Timeouts ([ADR 0044](0044-vertical-wave-slicing-contract-bounded-review-reply-first-handoff-heredoc-choke-and-worktree-process-reaping.md), [ADR 0048](0048-in-flight-wave-hardening-recurrence-guard-and-project-notebook.md)):** Write modular per-story/per-slice test files rather than growing a single monolithic test file; run inner-loop slice tests with explicit timeouts (`15s–30s`) before running the full cumulative Gate.
   - **Anti-Whack-a-Mole Law & Challenge Signals ([ADR 0029](0029-delegation-boundary-rolling-waves-and-challenge-reconciliation.md), [ADR 0044](0044-vertical-wave-slicing-contract-bounded-review-reply-first-handoff-heredoc-choke-and-worktree-process-reaping.md)):** After a 3rd failed fix with the same symptom, stop local patching to diagnose the root mechanism or commit completed independent work and escalate via `REOPEN_REQUEST`, `DEPENDENCY_REQUEST`, or `BLOCKED` (`agent_escalate_blocker`).
   - **Recurrence Guard Sibling Sweep on Fix Tasks ([ADR 0048](0048-in-flight-wave-hardening-recurrence-guard-and-project-notebook.md)):** When executing a `fix-*` task for a QA defect, sweep and fix all sibling components/routes/screens exhibiting the same root-cause pattern within the story's boundary and lock the fix with a regression test.
4. **QA Engineer (`qa`):**
   - **Mandatory Tier-2 Cross-Module Integration & Tier-3 Cold-Start Entrypoint Smoke ([ADR 0048](0048-in-flight-wave-hardening-recurrence-guard-and-project-notebook.md)):** Beyond checking PRD/Architecture traceability and unit/component assertions, `qa` must execute and record evidence of **Tier-2 Cross-Module Integration** (real caller → callee wiring across module seams without mocking the seam under test) and **Tier-3 Cold-Start Entrypoint Smoke** (booting the real CLI binary, HTTP server, or rendered UI entrypoint from a cold start with zero uncaught runtime/console/page errors) inside `qa-report.md` and the regression bundle before `qa.audit` can report `passed`.

## Consequences

- `CONTEXT.md` updates **Scrum role**, **Supervisor**, **Lead**, **Lead handoff**, **Zcode harness home**, **Helper MCP catalog**, **Seat kickoff packet**, **Wave Hardening**, and **Project notebook** to reflect Agile Scrum parity under ADR 0049.
- Both execution modes (`slp` and `scrum`) share identical clean-room ZCode isolation, Turn-1 preflight efficiency, Helper MCP catalog fidelity, and battle-tested operational defenses while preserving Scrum's 5-stage specialist workflow.
