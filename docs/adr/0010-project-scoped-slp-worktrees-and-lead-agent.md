# ADR 0010 — Project-scoped SLP seats (`cwd`), model-backed Lead agent, and automatic per-Task worktree landing

- **Status:** Accepted — 2026-10-15 (ratified by Andrew over a two-round `/grill-with-docs`, all-recommended; words "toàn bộ theo khuyến nghị của bạn")
- **Grounded in:** `research/project-start-harness-memory-mcp-skills.md` · `sting9k/seatworks` (`v3`, `plugin/server/desk/{project,slots,opening}.ts`, `plugin/content/records/notebook.md`) · `cuongntr/paseo-room` (`v0.9.0`/`v0.19.0`, `src/runtime-plugin/server/{git,controller}.ts`, `src/room/prompts/contract/{supervisor,lead,peer}.md`) · `sting8k/piggery` (`internal/core/{found,team,cwd}.go`) · `@getpaseo/server@0.11.1` (`agent-manager.js`, `paseo-tool-policy.js`, `providers/{claude,pi,codex}`)
- **Amends:** ADR 0006 §2–§3 (graduates `Lead` from a zero-model mechanical rider to a standing model-backed agent seat per project with intermediate task acceptance authority `accepted_by: "lead"`, while retaining the Driver's mechanical watch/respawn/ceiling underneath; moves `Supervisor` from a single `<home>-supervisor` directory to each project's root `cwd`) · ADR 0009 §2–§3 (adds `lead` to `lanes.json` and the `Seats & Models` lane switcher; scopes `Supervisor`, `Lead`, `Ledger`, `Reports`, `Retro`, and `Worktrees` per project via `gitCommonDir`)
- **Amended by:** [ADR 0024](0024-greenfield-project-bootstrap-and-project-deletion.md) (automatic Git bootstrap on greenfield directories when starting a seat or adding a Project, selectable missing-root Projects, and full `Project delete`)

## Decision

1. **Per-Project `Supervisor` and `Lead` rooted at the Project directory (`cwd = <project-root>`), never `<home>-supervisor`.**
   - Every Git repository opened in Paseo resolves its canonical project identity via `git rev-parse --show-toplevel --git-common-dir` to a deterministic `<project-slug> = <basename>-<sha1(gitRoot).slice(0,6)>`.
   - Each project has its own standing **Supervisor** (`labels: { seat: "supervisor", project: <slug> }`) and standing **Lead** (`labels: { seat: "lead", project: <slug> }`), both spawned with `cwd = <project-root>`.
   - Opening the Factory surface inside a workspace scopes automatically to that project's `<project-slug>`; opening from the global Factory screen provides a project selector before opening or dispatching to a project's Supervisor.
   - **Non-destructive role instruction injection:** The plugin never overwrites `<project-root>/AGENTS.md` or `<project-root>/CLAUDE.md`. Role contracts (`Supervisor`, `Lead`, `Agent`) are injected additively via Paseo's `server.before("agent.create")` / launch prompt/env surface so the underlying CLI harness (`claude`, `pi`, `codex`, `agy`) natively loads the repository's own `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `.mcp.json`, and project memory.

2. **True 3-Seat SLP Topology (`Owner ⇄ Supervisor ⇄ Lead ⇄ Agent`) with a dedicated `Lead` model lane.**
   - **`Supervisor` (`seat=supervisor`):** The sole conversational door to the Human Owner per project. Clarifies intent (`grilling`), agrees on the outcome and boundaries, dispatches directives to the project's `Lead`, relays `Needs-human` escalations and final completion summaries back to the Owner, and records Owner-ratified rules into `<project-root>/AGENTS.md` or `CONTEXT.md`.
   - **`Lead` (`seat=lead`):** A standing model-backed Paseo agent per project with its own selectable lane in `Lane config` (`lanes.lead`, recommended tier `Reasoning / Pro`, added as the 5th lane card on `Seats & Models`) and a **`Rotate / Revoke Lead`** action when context rotation is needed. The Lead reads the repository, decomposes directives into one or more `Task`s, sets each `Contract` (`gate`, `artifact`, `scope`, `skills`), invokes the `Driver` (`factory run`), inspects `Verdict` / `Fresh-eyes` / `Watch` / `Report` evidence, re-briefs or re-pokes red/stalled work, and reports completion to the `Supervisor`.
   - **Mechanical instrumentation stays underneath the `Lead`:** The `Driver`'s deterministic loop (`Life signal`, `Denial-stall` re-poke, `Substrate death` classification, one automatic `Respawn`, and `Attempt ceiling = 3`) remains active as the `Lead`'s automated instrument ("mechanize under the seats, never remove the seat").

3. **Acceptance authority in 3-Seat SLP (`accepted_by: "owner" | "supervisor" | "lead"`).**
   - In accordance with canonical SLP (*"Among agents, Lead alone accepts; Human retains override"*), the `Lead` may call `factory accept` (`accepted_by: "lead"`) on an intermediate `Task` whose `Attempt` has a **green `Verdict`** and **either a completed `Fresh-eyes CLEAR` or fresh-eyes off**, allowing multi-task plans (`Task 1 → Task 2 → Task 3`) to land sequentially without blocking on human clicks mid-chain.
   - **Clarified 2026-10-23, `grill-with-docs` Q5:** When the Contract enables Fresh-eyes, a pending, failed, timed-out or interrupted review is not CLEAR and does not authorize Lead acceptance. This includes a missing review after daemon restart. The Owner, or the Supervisor acting on the Owner's word, retains override on a green Attempt; Watch remains advisory.
   - Whenever `Fresh-eyes` raises a `CONCERN` or a `Task` hits `Needs-human` (`attempt-ceiling`, `died-after-respawn`, `denied-again`, `silent-after-repoke`), the `Lead` may not self-accept; it must either spawn a corrective attempt within the ceiling or escalate to the `Supervisor` / `Owner`.
   - The `Owner` retains override of the Lead-specific restrictions on a green Attempt through the Factory surface (`Accept #n` button, `accepted_by: "owner"`) and final sign-off through the `Supervisor` (`accepted_by: "supervisor"` on the Owner's word). Every `attempt_accepted` event in the Ledger records `accepted_by`.

4. **Automatic per-Task `git worktree` isolation and local landing on `Accept`.**
   - While `Supervisor` and `Lead` sit in `<project-root>`, every spawned `Agent` (Worker) runs inside an isolated linked `git worktree` managed by the Factory under `<projectStateDir>/worktrees/<task>` on branch `factory/<task>` rooted at the Contract's `base` commit (copying `.worktreeinclude` files when present).
   - The `Agent` edits, `Claim`s, and runs the `Gate` inside its dedicated worktree, keeping `<project-root>` clean at all times and eliminating single-checkout `Workspace conflict`s between `Supervisor`/`Lead` and concurrent `Task`s.
   - **Landing on `Accept`:** When a green `Attempt` is accepted (`owner`, `supervisor`, or `lead`), the Factory integrates `claimedCommit` into `<project-root>`'s local branch (fast-forward or clean merge/cherry-pick) and removes the temporary worktree. If `<project-root>` has conflicting uncommitted edits or a merge conflict, `attempt_accepted` still records in the Ledger while preserving branch `factory/<task>` and reporting the conflict for `Lead`/`Supervisor` resolution. External `git push` to remote remains permanently a human-authorized act.

5. **Per-Project Factory State (`<stateDir>/projects/<project-slug>/`) with Machine + Project `Lane config`.**
   - Each project stores its isolated `ledger.jsonl`, `report-<task>-<n>.md`, `gate-<task>-<n>.log`, `retro-<date>.md`, and `worktrees/` under `<stateDir>/projects/<project-slug>/`, preventing cross-project pollution in `Tasks` and `Retro`.
   - `<stateDir>/lanes.json` provides the machine-wide default lanes (`supervisor`, `lead`, `worker`, `watch`, `retro`, `freshEyes`), with optional per-project overrides in `<stateDir>/projects/<project-slug>/lanes.json`.

6. **Two-Tier Project Memory & Native Harness MCP / Skill Preservation.**
   - **Memory write fence:** Contracted working `Agent`s (`Peer`s) are forbidden by the `Choke policy` / scope verification from modifying `<project-root>/AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, or `CONTEXT.md` (unless a Task's Contract explicitly scopes documentation maintenance), preventing unverified worker hallucinations from becoming repo law.
   - **Immediate + Ratified memory:** Only `Supervisor` and `Lead` (either immediately upon discovering/receiving a project rule in conversation, or when ratifying a `Retro` `Proposal` from the UI/chat) update `<project-root>/AGENTS.md` or `CONTEXT.md`. Because all future worktrees branch from `<project-root>`, every subsequent `Agent` inherits the updated rules automatically.
   - **MCP & Skills:** Because Paseo drives the user's installed CLI harnesses (`claude`, `pi`, `codex`, `agy`), every seat retains access to the operator's configured MCP servers (`~/.claude.json`, `<repo>/.mcp.json`, `~/.pi/agent/mcp.json`, `<repo>/.pi/mcp.json`, `~/.codex/config.toml`, `~/.gemini/antigravity/mcp/`) and shared engineering skills (`~/.agents/skills/`, `~/.claude/skills/`, `~/.gemini/config/skills/`). For `pi` lanes, ADR 0011 isolates each role in a clean-room `PI_CODING_AGENT_DIR` (`<stateDir>/pi-agent/<role>`) that strips user TUI packages/extensions and `APPEND_SYSTEM.md`, uses Pi v1.3.0's native `builtin:mcp`, and filters `<available_skills>` on working `Agent`s to their 3 core skills plus on-demand `Contract` skills. Orchestration tools (`paseoTools` / `factory` CLI) are withheld from working `Agent`s.
   - **Role skill index & Contract `skills`:** Each role prompt ends with a 1-line trigger index (`Supervisor`: `grilling`, `domain-modeling`; `Lead`: `codebase-design`, `domain-modeling`, `tdd`; `Agent`: `tdd`, `diagnosing-bugs`, `codebase-design`), and `Contract` gains an optional `skills?: string[]` field so the `Lead` can mandate which `SKILL.md` files the working `Agent` must open first.

## Consequences

- `CONTEXT.md` updates **Supervisor**, **Lead**, **Workspace**, **Workspace conflict**, **Accepted**, **Factory surface**, and **Lane config**, and adds **Project** and **Task worktree**.
- `lanes.json` and the `Seats & Models` UI expand to 5 selectable lanes: `Supervisor`, `Lead`, `Default Worker`, `Watch & Retro`, and `Fresh-eyes`.
- `Supervisor` in `<home>-supervisor` is retired in favor of per-project `Supervisor` and `Lead` seats rooted at `<project-root>`.
