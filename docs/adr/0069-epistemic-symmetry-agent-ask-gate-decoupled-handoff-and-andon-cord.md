# 0069 — Epistemic Symmetry, Topology-Blind `agent_ask`, Gate-Decoupled Reopen/Partial Handoffs, Automated Andon Cord, and Supervisor Loop Health

- **Date:** 2026-11-23
- **Status:** Accepted — ratified by the Owner across `/grill-me` rounds 1–3 (Q1–Q15; implementation spec `.scratch/epistemic-symmetry-and-andon-cord/spec.md`).
- **Grounded in:** Live telemetry audit across 7 active Paseo Desktop Projects (`2026-11-01`–`2026-11-23`: `655` unique Tasks, `913` `contract_set`, `748` `claim_reported`, `99 / 100` `BLOCKED (context-ceiling)` events with zero prior challenge, `0 / 96` `peer_challenge` and `84 / 84` `complete` claims across `2026-11-21..23`, `GP-R24c4` auto-handoff race, `GP-T28l` `it.skip` partial hack, `GP-P2c2..c2c` 3× ceiling cascade) · primary-source synthesis in `research/lead-peer-symmetry-and-negotiation.md` · `cuongntr/paseo-room` (`v0.19.0`, `src/room/prompts/contract/peer.md`, `src/runtime-plugin/server/contracts/peer.ts`) · `src/{events,factory,mcp/shared,roles-contract,disposition-prompt,task-status,agy-mcp-schema-heal}.ts` · `plugin/bin/{bridge,driver}.mjs` · `plugin/server/{spool,peer-handoff,peer-in-flight,lead-mailbox,roles}.ts`.
- **Amends:**
  - [ADR 0053](0053-canonical-slp-core-provisional-briefs-peer-dispositions-live-reconciliation-and-six-field-handoff.md) (promotes `agent_ask` + `lead_reply_peer` over `agent_escalate_blocker` / `lead_repoke_agent`, persists durable `peer_asked` and `repoke_dispatched` ruling metadata in the Ledger, decouples non-`complete` claims from shell Gate execution, and enforces topology-blind Peer prompts).
  - [ADR 0054](0054-canonical-slp-contract-gate-effort-and-task-retirement.md) (adds optional `reopenConditions` on `lead_set_contract` and removes `"immutable Contract"` wording from the MCP description).
  - [ADR 0057](0057-every-seat-replacement-is-a-handoff-supervisor-handoff-and-wide-colourful-factory-tab.md) (adds mid-context Andon Checkpoint at ~50% of Peer handoff mark, blocks Peer auto-handoff while an unanswered `peer_asked` awaits Lead, and restricts `200k` Peer-2 auto-handoff to `engineer` Tasks with verified worktree progress).
  - [ADR 0058](0058-a-peer-task-closes-only-on-an-outcome-not-on-idle.md) (preserves the idle-Peer hold on unanswered `peer_asked` and non-`complete` claim handbacks without nudging).
  - [ADR 0068](0068-daemon-gate-queue-async-claim-and-factory-overview.md) (removes the `"never notify Lead directly"` prohibition from `formatPeerRedWakeupText` on `RED` Attempts `1..2`, replacing it with explicit two-branch fix-vs-challenge guidance).

## Context

Even after [ADR 0053](0053-canonical-slp-core-provisional-briefs-peer-dispositions-live-reconciliation-and-six-field-handoff.md) and [ADR 0054](0054-canonical-slp-contract-gate-effort-and-task-retirement.md) introduced in-place re-contracting (`resetPeer: false`), 4 Peer dispositions, and message-bearing `lead_repoke_agent`, a forensic audit of live runs through `v0.20.5` (`research/lead-peer-symmetry-and-negotiation.md` §2 & §7) revealed that `Lead ↔ Peer` dialogue remained heavily skewed toward silent compliance:

1. **Zero Peer pushback in recent runs & 99% silent context-ceiling exhaustion:** Across `2026-11-17..23`, **`99 of 100`** `BLOCKED (context-ceiling)` events burned `2 × 200k` tokens with zero prior `peer_challenge` and zero prior claims (including a 3× consecutive cascade on `goal-partner-f17f4d` `GP-P2c2 → GP-P2c2b → GP-P2c2c` on `v0.20.5`, burning ~1.2M tokens). In `2026-11-21..23` (`96` tasks), `peer_challenge` dropped to **`0 / 96` (`0.0%`)**, even though earlier challenges succeeded in-place on `8 / 10` cases (median Lead turnaround ~74s).
2. **Tool framing & user-turn recency suppress dissent:** `lead_set_contract` still describes `"an immutable Contract"`, the Peer's sole pushback tool is named `agent_escalate_blocker` (described as `"unresolvable blocker"` and prefixed `[Blocker on task ...]`), initial user prompts command `"Execute task ... call agent_claim"` without mentioning premise verification or pushback, and Peer prompts leak full Factory orchestration topology (`Owner`, `Supervisor`, `Driver`, `Factory CLI`, `GateQueue`), reinforcing an authority gradient.
3. **`RED` Attempt `1..2` wakeup actively forbids challenging a broken Gate (`ADR 0068` regression):** Because `ADR 0068` forbade Peers from pre-running the full cumulative Gate during TDD, a broken Contract/Gate on `HEAD` is now discovered only after `agent_claim` returns `RED` Attempt `1`. Yet `formatPeerRedWakeupText` (`plugin/server/spool.ts:1985`) explicitly orders `(never notify Lead directly — Lead wakes on GREEN or at attempt 3)`, directly nullifying `REOPEN_REQUEST (verification)` and pressuring Peers to hack tests or exhaust all 3 Attempts.
4. **`agent_claim` forces non-`complete` outcomes through `GateQueue`:** `499 of 500` post-0053 Peer claims (`84 / 84` in Nov 21–23) were forced into `outcome: "complete"` (`0` `reopen`, `0` `blocked`, `1` `partial` that had to `it.skip` 3 tests in `GP-T28l`) because calling `agent_claim` on `engineer` with `reopen`, `blocked`, or `partial` still required a commit SHA, ran the shell Gate, returned `RED`, and burned an Attempt.
5. **Peer Auto-Handoff races pending `peer_challenge` & lacks observability:** In `GP-R24c4`, Auto-Handoff spawned a 2nd Peer on a known-broken Brief 29 seconds after Peer filed `REOPEN_REQUEST (API)`, wasting `211k` tokens. Meanwhile, `peer_challenge` lived only in `mailbox.jsonl` (lost on mailbox resets) and `repoke_dispatched` dropped `message`, leaving `supervisor_read_ledger` blind to authority-gradient patterns.

## Decision

1. **Preserve the GREEN-Only Verification Core & Single-Phase Execution (`Q1`, `Q4`):**
   - Keep `paseo-factory`'s core invariant intact: only an `outcome: "complete"` Attempt with a `GREEN` `Verdict` may land via `lead_merge` (no Red-Gate Override). Broken Gates or flawed premises are reconciled by Peer pushback → Lead in-place `lead_set_contract` (`resetPeer: false`) → genuine `GREEN`.
   - Keep single-phase `engineer` execution by default (no Layer-2 `preflight → writable` lock state machine in this release), pairing `reopenConditions` + user-turn premise reminders + `RED` Attempt `1..2` pushback unlock with existing read-only `scout`/`architect` dispositions.

2. **Topology-Blind Peer ACI: `agent_ask`, `lead_reply_peer`, Optional `reopenConditions`, & Prompt Hygiene (`Q2`, `Q8`, `Q14`, `Q15`):**
   - Introduce **`agent_ask`** as the Peer's primary feedback/challenge MCP tool (paired cleanly with `agent_claim`) and **`lead_reply_peer`** as the Lead's primary live reconciliation tool; keep `agent_escalate_blocker` and `lead_repoke_agent` as hidden backward-compatible aliases in `bridge.mjs` / Spool / schema-heal handlers (not advertised as the primary choice to new sessions).
   - **`agent_ask` carries zero topology arguments** (no `task`, `project`, `workspace`, or `recipient` — bound strictly server-side from correlation identity) and uses a compact PACE + CONTRA schema:
     - `{ kind: "reopen_premise" | "gate_mismatch" | "dependency" | "clarify" | "blocked", layer?: "foundation" | "dependency" | "lifecycle" | "API" | "ownership" | "verification", summary: string, evidence: string, proposal?: string }` (`layer` mandatory when `kind === "reopen_premise"`).
     - Tool description enforces CONTRA selectivity: use when code/spec contradicts the Brief or Gate, a prerequisite is missing, or an ambiguity changes code behavior — never for routine implementation details the Peer can decide independently.
     - Remove the `[Blocker on task ...]` prefix in `bridge.mjs` in favor of a neutral kind tag (`[Peer <KIND> (<layer>) on <task>]`), and remove `"immutable Contract"` from `lead_set_contract`.
   - Add optional **`reopenConditions?: string[]`** to `lead_set_contract` (`contract_set` Ledger event, CLI, Spool). When provided, render prominently as `Reopen if: ...` in the Peer brief; when omitted, initial user prompts still end with an explicit premise/Gate verification + `agent_ask` reminder.
   - **Topology-Blind Peer Hygiene:** Remove `Owner`, `Supervisor`, `Driver`, `Factory CLI`, `GateQueue`, and `"paseo-factory verification loop"` narration from Peer system and turn prompts (`src/roles-contract.ts`, `plugin/bin/bridge.mjs`, `plugin/bin/driver.mjs`, `src/disposition-prompt.ts`, `plugin/server/peer-handoff.ts`, `formatPeerRedWakeupText`), framing the Peer neutrally as an independent engineer working a provisional brief with `agent_ask` and `agent_claim` while retaining legacy idempotency markers in `plugin/server/roles.ts` so legacy sessions never receive duplicate contracts.

3. **Gate-Decoupled `reopen` / `blocked` / `partial` Claims & Two-Branch `RED` Attempt `1..2` Wakeup (`Q3`, `Q9`, `Q10`):**
   - In `admitClaim` (`src/factory.ts`) and Spool `claim` (`plugin/server/spool.ts`), any `agent_claim` with `outcome: "reopen" | "blocked" | "partial"` **bypasses `GateQueue` completely**:
     - Runs no shell Gate, opens/burns no Attempt (`1..3`), emits no `RED` verdict, and is never eligible for `lead_merge`.
     - `reopen` and `blocked` require no new commit SHA; `partial` validates the supplied commit SHA (or clean worktree `HEAD` if committed) when present.
     - Persists the full 6-Field Handoff to `report-<task>-ask-<seq>.md`, appends `peer_asked` (`source: "agent_claim"`) to the Ledger, keeps the Peer session alive and paused (no idle-nudge per ADR 0058), and delivers the handoff to `Lead mailbox` so Lead can narrow the Contract in-place or split/close the Task.
   - Fix `formatPeerRedWakeupText` (`plugin/server/spool.ts:1985`): retain direct Peer wakeup on `RED` Attempts `1..2`, remove `(never notify Lead directly — ...)`, and instruct two explicit branches: (a) if the failure is in your code/tests, fix, commit, and call `agent_claim`; (b) if the failure stems from a broken/stale Gate or contradictory Brief, **never weaken or skip tests** — call `agent_ask` (`gate_mismatch` or `reopen_premise`) immediately.

4. **Automated Andon Cord, Peer-Handoff Choke, & Preserved Lead-Handoff Anti-Deadlock Law (`Q5`, `Q7`, `Q11`):**
   - **Choke Peer Auto-Handoff on unanswered `peer_asked` (`GP-R24c4` fix):** In `plugin/server/peer-handoff.ts`, `eligible()` returns `false` whenever the Task's Ledger epoch contains a `peer_asked` newer than the latest `repoke_dispatched` or `contract_set`.
   - **Preserve Lead Handoff's anti-deadlock law (`Q7`):** Do **not** choke Lead Handoff when a `peer_asked` / `peer_challenge` arrives while Lead is already in `rotation_pending` (>160k). Because `rotation_pending` holds mailbox delivery, letting Lead rotate cleanly at that quiet point avoids deadlock and delivers `[handover + peer_asked]` directly to the fresh Successor Lead on Turn 1 while the Peer stays alive and paused. (When Lead is `running` or after `lead_reply_peer`, `projectPeerInFlight` already holds Lead rotation.)
   - **Mid-Context Andon Checkpoint (~50% of Peer handoff mark):** At `turn_ended` only (*Sterile Cockpit*, never mid-tool), if an unsettled Peer crosses `50%` of its `handoffMarkTokens("peer", maxTokens)` (`100k` on a `200k` mark) with zero `claim_reported` and zero `peer_asked` in the current Contract epoch, inject **at most one** concise re-anchoring nudge asking Peer to verify whether Brief premises, Gate assumptions, or wave scope are flawed and to call `agent_ask` before continuing.
   - **Progress-Gated Peer-2 Auto-Handoff at `200k`:** At the Peer context mark (`200k`), allow automatic successor Peer-2 spawning **only** when `disposition === "engineer"` AND the `Task worktree` shows real progress (at least one commit ahead of `contract.base` or a non-empty code diff). For read-only dispositions (`scout`, `architect`, `reviewer`) or zero-progress `engineer` sessions, halt immediately at `200k` and notify Lead (`BLOCKED (context-ceiling)`) with the worktree/transcript digest so Lead splits or revises the Contract instead of burning a second blind `200k` session.

5. **Durable Ledger Audit (`peer_asked` + `repoke_dispatched` Ruling), Supervisor `Loop Health`, & Anonymized Devil's Advocate Reviewer (`Q6`, `Q12`, `Q13`):**
   - Add v2 Ledger event **`peer_asked`** (`{ event: "peer_asked", task, kind, layer?, summary, source: "agent_ask" | "agent_claim" | "legacy_blocker", agent? }`) and extend **`repoke_dispatched`** with backward-compatible optional fields `kind?: "denial-stall" | "ruling"` and `message?: string` (truncated to `<= 1,000` chars). All existing v2 Ledger files replay without migration.
   - Append a compact deterministic **`Loop Health`** block (3–4 lines, zero LLM calls) to `supervisor_read_ledger` reporting: `peer_asked` count/rate, `context-ceiling` stops with `0` prior `peer_asked`, `complete` claims with genuine `unknowns` gaps (explicitly filtering ADR 0068 `"deferred/skipped to daemon GateQueue"` boilerplate), and `red` attempts / `re-contracts`.
   - Harden `disposition: "reviewer"` (`src/roles-contract.ts`, `src/disposition-prompt.ts`): Lead supplies **Outcome + Spec/Invariants + Diff/Commit** only (never embedding Lead/Peer rationalizations or pre-baked conclusions), and Reviewer adopts an evidence-backed **Devil's Advocate** stance (identifying the strongest weakness with `file:line` evidence before agreeing, without manufacturing objections when evidence holds).

## Considered Options

- **Allow Red-Gate Override on `lead_accept_task` (`paseo-room` D6):** Rejected (`Q1`) — `paseo-factory` already supports in-place `lead_set_contract` (`resetPeer: false`), so fixing a broken Gate and reaching a genuine `GREEN` preserves the `HEAD`-always-green invariant without introducing override loopholes.
- **Name the Peer tool bare `ask` (`paseo-room` verbatim):** Rejected (`Q15`) — bare `ask` collides conceptually with host built-in human-question tools (`ask_question`, `AskUserQuestion`, `question`) and internally with Spool's `kind: "ask"` permission choke, looks asymmetric beside `agent_claim`, and encourages passive over-questioning; `agent_ask` paired with CONTRA description and assertive `kind` ordering avoids all four pitfalls.
- **Two-Phase Layer-2 Pre-Flight Lock (`preflight → writable`):** Deferred (`Q4`) — Layer-1 provider fences (`disallowedTools`, OpenCode `permission.edit`) are fixed at session creation (`agent.create.before`), so dynamic mid-session unlocking adds multi-provider Layer-2 state complexity before measuring the impact of `agent_ask`, `reopenConditions`, and the `RED` wakeup fix.
- **Choke Lead Handoff while `peer_asked` is queued:** Rejected (`Q7`) — `rotation_pending` blocks `drainLeadMailbox`, so holding `beginHandoff` on a queued `peer_asked` would deadlock; letting the over-limit Lead rotate cleanly hands the queued question to a fresh Successor Lead immediately on Turn 1.

## Consequences

- Peers gain a low-friction, topology-blind, non-punitive channel (`agent_ask`) plus safe `GateQueue`-bypassing `reopen`/`blocked`/`partial` claims, eliminating the `ADR 0068` `RED` Attempt `1..2` gag order and removing the incentive to `it.skip` tests.
- Automated Andon Checkpoint (~50% mark), `peer_asked` Peer-handoff choke, and progress-gated Peer-2 spawning directly cut silent `400k`-token `context-ceiling` waste without interrupting active tool calls (*Sterile Cockpit*).
- Durable `peer_asked` + `repoke_dispatched` ruling records survive `mailbox.jsonl` resets and power zero-token `Loop Health` auditing in `supervisor_read_ledger`.
- `CONTEXT.md` is updated alongside implementation to reflect `agent_ask`, `lead_reply_peer`, `reopenConditions`, `peer_asked`, `Andon Checkpoint`, and `Loop Health`.
